# Μάθησις: everything the site does

## Reading
- **Two-column reader.** Greek sits beside an aligned English translation. You can show both, Greek only or English only.
- **Texts.** The library covers a large catalogue of Ancient Greek authors and works, with about 1,800 works. Texts load from GitHub, from a folder on your computer, from a downloaded ZIP or from a web address you choose.
- **Passage references.**
  - Every passage has a standard citation, and clicking its number copies it.
  - Line numbers appear every five lines, along with speaker labels and page or line markers.
  - Verse lines shrink to fit the column.
  - The first passage of each part gets a decorative initial.
- **Word lookup.** Click any Greek word to look it up in Logeion, Perseus or Wiktionary. Keyboard users can move between words with the arrow keys.
- **Choosing editions.** You can pick which Greek edition and which translation to show. A note tells you how much of the work has a translation.
- **Moving around.** There are part and page steppers and previous/next buttons.

## Study lenses (a side panel in the reader)
- **Echoes:** where the same phrasing appears elsewhere, plus your own links.
- **Words:** which word stems recur, by passage and by era.
- **Meter:** scansion of hexameter, elegiac pentameter and iambic trimeter, with audio playback.
- **Map:** places named in the translation, plotted on a map.
- **Manuscript:** capital-letter and lower-case re-settings of the passage, plus links to manuscript images.

## Finding things
- **Header search.**
  - It ignores accents, breathings and case.
  - It jumps straight to passages (for example "Iliad 1.33").
  - It searches texts, authors, your own library, the guide and wiki, and a Greek word lookup.
  - Recent searches are remembered.
- **Library page.** You can sort by author, title or era, filter by letter, era, translation and genre, and get "where to start" suggestions.
- **Home page.**
  - A "New to Greek? Start in Study" band comes first.
  - Below it are Continue reading, the hero, a passage of the day, and reading paths.
  - Further down are a Greek-through-the-centuries chart, a corpus section, a wiki card, offline setup and sources.

## Learning (Study)
- **Start here guide.**
  - Eight steps: the alphabet and sounds, vowels, breathings and accents, dictionary forms, looking up a word, and reading word studies.
  - Links in the guide open the exact passage in the reader.
- **The alphabet at a glance** and **five things to know about reading Greek.**
- **Practise:** short "just for fun" lessons on the alphabet, noun declensions, sounds, the Iliad and myth. They have exercises, audio and a progress record.
- **"On learning":** an essay with quotations from the philosophers.

## Wiki
- **Authors.** Author pages have a timeline, works, editions, notes and a Wikipedia summary. You can filter authors by era or genre.
- **Eras.** The eras pages have a chart drawn to scale.
- **Articles.** Wiki articles cover manuscripts and transmission, textual variants, and editions and translations. There are also work articles (the Iliad so far).
- **Everyday life.** These are scholar-written articles with checked quotations that link to the passages.

## Your own library ("My library")
- **Favourites:** favourite works.
- **Bookmarks:** bookmarks with notes, `#tags` and links between passages.
- **Author notes.**
- **Saved words,** with spaced-repetition review.
- **Saved places** from the Map lens.
- **Back-up:** export and import as a file.

## Forum and accounts (built but switched off on the live site)
- **Accounts:** sign in with an emailed code, sync your library across devices, and delete your account.
- **Boards:** general boards, bug reports, suggestions, and threads about a passage.
- **Safety:** reporting, hiding people, a moderator queue and community rules.
- **Status:** these become active when you finish the Supabase setup and set the switch (repository variable `ACCOUNTS`) to on.

## Settings and comfort
- Text size and line spacing, serif or sans typeface, and light, dark or automatic theme.
- Text source (online, this computer or a web address).
- The site works on phones, with a bottom tab bar, a full-screen search and folding home sections.
- A Notes panel is available.

## Site-wide
- **Web addresses.** The site uses real page addresses, and old `#` links still work.
- **Share previews.** About 2,240 pages have their own share preview, and there is a sitemap.
- **Pages:** an About page with credits, a Privacy page, a Not found page, and the site's own writing under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Separately from the site, there is a private review page and a private notes file that stays out of GitHub.
